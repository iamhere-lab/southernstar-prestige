Frequently Asked Questions

What homes are available at Prestige Southern Star?

3 and 4 BHK residences from 1,800 to 2,774 sq ft: 3 BHK + 3T (1,800 to 1,820 sq ft), 3 BHK + 3T + home office (2,083 to 2,118 sq ft) and 4 BHK + 4T + home office (2,690 to 2,774 sq ft).

What is the starting price?

3 BHK + 3T homes start at ₹2.36 Cr*, 3 BHK + 3T + home office at ₹2.75 Cr* and 4 BHK at ₹3.55 Cr*. Prices are indicative and exclude taxes and statutory charges. Ask for the tower-wise price sheet for the exact cost of a unit.

Where is Prestige Southern Star located?

In Akshayanagar, off Bannerghatta Road, South Bengaluru. Singasandra Metro Station is 3 km away, Hulimavu Metro Station 4 km, Electronic City 5 km, JP Nagar 5.4 km and Koramangala 6.6 km, as per the project brochure.

Is the project RERA registered?

A landscaped central park, three swimming pools including an Olympic-length pool, an expansive clubhouse with a mini theatre and gaming arcade, and a one-acre fitness district with courts for tennis, badminton, squash, basketball and more.
